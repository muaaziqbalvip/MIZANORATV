Privacy Policy

Mizanora TV aapki privacy ko tehzeeb se treat karta hai. Hum sirf wo data collect karte hain jo service chalane ke liye zaroori hai.

Hum kya collect karte hain

  • Account data (admin): email address — Firebase Authentication ke through, sirf panel login.
  • Channel & config data: aap jo channels/servers add karte hain — Firestore me.
  • Technical logs: serverless request logs (IP, user-agent, status) — security aur debugging ke liye, Vercel par.
  • Browser storage: theme/preferences aur service worker cache (local, aapke device par).

Hum kya NAHI karte

  • Koi selling ya renting of personal data.
  • Koi third-party ad-tracking pixel (ads sirf aapke apne configure kiye hue pre-roll hote hain).
  • Streaming history ka permanent storage.

Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google) — auth + database
  • Vercel — hosting + serverless
  • GitHub — master playlist storage (agar aapne connect kiya ho)

Aapke rights

Data deletion ke liye email karein — hum 7 working days me delete kar dete hain.

DMCA / Copyright

Mizanora TV khud koi copyrighted media host nahi karta. Channels sirf third-party URLs ke references hote hain jo owner ne add kiye hote hain.

Agar aapko lagta hai ke koi content aapke copyright ki khilaf warzi kar raha hai, to contact@mizanora.tv par ye details bhejein:

  • Aapka naam, contact aur capacity (owner/agent)
  • Copyrighted work ki details
  • Exact URL/location jahan violation hai
  • Good-faith statement aur signature

Valid notice milne par channel/source 48 hours me remove kar diya jata hai.
